Why Pahad Organics

Why buy from Pahad

Farm-direct, no middlemen

We collect produce straight from farmer groups and women self-help groups in Himachal Pradesh, so freshness stays in and layers of middlemen stay out.

Homemade & small-batch

Ghee, peanut butter and more are made at home in small batches - the way hill families have always made them, with nothing artificial added.

Fair prices for farmers

Pahad Organics is a livelihood enterprise of PAHAD Trust. Every order pays hill farmers fairly and keeps traditional seeds and farming alive.
