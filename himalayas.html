From the Himalayas

Why food from high hills tastes different.

Clean air, cold nights, mineral-rich soil and seeds saved for generations. Our produce is grown the slow, natural way - and it shows in the taste.

Chamba

Home of our rajma, millets and fresh akhrot (walnuts). Chamba's farmers still grow traditional varieties, saved and exchanged through community seed banks.
