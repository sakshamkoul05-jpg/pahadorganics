Our Story

Grown in the hills. Rooted in trust.

PAHAD Trust (Professional Assistance for Hill Area Development) is a grassroots, public charitable organisation established in 2018 in Himachal Pradesh.

We work at the intersection of climate-resilient agriculture, sustainable livelihoods, traditional knowledge systems and participatory local governance - enabling communities to lead their own development.

Pahad Organics is our livelihood enterprise. It supports farmers in marketing their organic and natural produce, strengthens local value chains and improves rural incomes - so chemical-free food reaches your table at a fair price for the farmer.

Our journey

2018

PAHAD Trust is established in Dharamshala, Himachal Pradesh, with a vision of resilient, self-reliant Himalayan communities.

SAFAL

Our flagship programme - Sustainable Agriculture for Farmers' Livelihood - trains farmers in composting, vermicomposting, natural pest management and seed conservation.

Seed Revival

The Chamba Beej Khojo Abhiyan identifies local seed saviours and builds community seed banks. Today 100+ traditional seed varieties are conserved.

Pahad Organics

Our social enterprise takes hill produce to market - millets, rajma, honey, ghee, teas and more - ensuring fair prices and promoting indigenous varieties.

Today

2000+ farmers engaged across 50+ villages, with 25+ women self-help groups at the centre of the work.

Led by Ritu Sharma

Pahad Organics is led by Ritu Sharma, Team Lead at PAHAD Trust, who coordinates the SAFAL programme, seed bank initiatives and farmer training - and works directly with the women farmers who grow your food.
